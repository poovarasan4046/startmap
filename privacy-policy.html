Privacy Policy for Starmap

Effective Date: April 30, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Starmap ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects the personal information of users ("you", "your") when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Starmap, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: such as your name, phone number, and email address when you sign up or log in.
  • Location Data: precise GPS location data to enable location tracking and display real-time user positions on the map.
  • Device Information: including your device ID, operating system, and crash logs for diagnostics.
  • Usage Data: interactions with the app such as task management, feature usage, and preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve Starmap’s functionality
  • To track user location and manage real-time updates
  • To provide personalized user experience and role-based access
  • To communicate updates, respond to inquiries, and provide support
  • To analyze app performance and resolve technical issues

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share limited information with:

  • Trusted third-party services (e.g., hosting, analytics, authentication) under strict confidentiality agreements
  • Authorities if required by law, legal process, or governmental request

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption and secure storage. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can request to access, update, or delete your personal information by contacting us
  • You can disable location tracking from your device settings, though this may limit app functionality

6. Children’s Privacy

Starmap is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
